Limerick Rules

  • Five lines long.
  • Lines 1, 2, 5 rhyme (A) & have ~8-9 syllables.
  • Lines 3, 4 rhyme (B) & have ~5-6 syllables.
  • Follows a specific rhythm (often anapestic).
  • Usually humorous or nonsensical.

Common opening: "There once was a..."

Example Limerick

There once was a man from Nantucket
Who kept all his cash in a bucket.
But his daughter, named Nan,
Ran away with a man,
And as for the bucket, Nantucket.

- Anonymous
